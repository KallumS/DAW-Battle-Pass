Installing Writing Battle Pass
==============================

1. Drag Writing Battle Pass into the Applications folder (the shortcut
   beside it).

2. Open it from Applications. The first time, the Mac says it can't check
   the app for malicious software, because it isn't from the App Store or a
   paid Apple developer. Click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy &
   Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Writing Battle
   Pass. You only do this once.

   If the Mac says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", open Terminal
   (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

       xattr -cr "/Applications/Writing Battle Pass.app"

   then open it again. That removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark,
   which is all the warning is about.

3. To earn XP for every letter you type, click "Allow typing to count" on
   the welcome screen and turn on Writing Battle Pass in System Settings >
   Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring. It only counts key presses - it
   never reads, keeps or sends what you write. Quit and reopen it if it
   doesn't notice straight away. After an update you may need to switch it
   off and on again there.

Closing the window doesn't stop the clock: the pencil in the menu bar at
the top of the screen keeps counting. Quit from there to stop it.
