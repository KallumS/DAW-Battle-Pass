# 0014 - Seasons are calendar months; days start at 4am

Fortnite's seasons run about ten weeks. Here a season is a calendar month,
numbered from January 2026 (`L.seasonId`), because a month is a length
everyone keeps in their head and "ends in 12 days" needs no explaining. A
hundred tiers is 25 active hours - under an hour a day - with bonus tiers
for anyone who goes past it.

A day (check-in, daily quests, daily deals, the history) starts at 4am, not
midnight, so a session that runs past midnight still belongs to the evening
it started. A week is named for its Monday. Both come from
`G.DAY_START_HOUR`.
