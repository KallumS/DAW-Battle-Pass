# 0007 - Seven rarities, Poor to Mythic

The user asked for the ladder of loot games - poor, common, rare, epic,
legendary, mythic - with Diablo as an inspiration. Uncommon goes between
Common and Rare as in WoW and Fortnite, making seven.

Poor is real: grey junk ("Untitled Beat 37", "Cracked Earbuds") worth almost
nothing, because junk is what makes a Legendary feel like one. Rarity changes
the thing itself, not only its colour: more chords and richer harmony, more
bars, more affixes, a power. The colours are in `docs/COLOUR.md`; Mythic is
pink rather than Fortnite's gold to keep clear of the accent.
