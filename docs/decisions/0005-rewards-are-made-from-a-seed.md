# 0005 - Rewards are made from a seed

Nothing the pass hands out is stored in a list. Ideas, gear, titles and
banners are made by generators from word lists and musical rules, which is
what makes them endless.

The dice are Midi Variator's Park-Miller generator, as in Good Idea. A tier's
rewards are `L.tierRewards(season, tier, ilvl)`, seeded from the season and
the tier alone, so the track shows what each tier holds before you reach it -
the anticipation is half of a battle pass. Live drops (crates, rerolls) use
the session's dice. Daily deals are made once a day and saved, so levelling
up does not change them under you.

Randomness is only ever a choice among meaningful options: chords from the
progressions everyone knows or a tonic-predominant-dominant walk, melody
notes in the key, drum patterns from real styles with variations.
