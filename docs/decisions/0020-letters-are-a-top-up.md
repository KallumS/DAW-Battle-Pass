# 0020 - A letter is a tenth of an XP, capped a minute

She asked that typing give XP "but not scaled too highly". A letter is 0.1
XP; a thousand words (about 5,500 letters) is about 550 XP - about what two
hours at the desk earns from time alone, and roughly a level in the early
game. Base typing XP is capped at 30 a minute (300 letters, about 60 words a
minute sustained), so holding a key or mashing the keyboard can't farm
levels. Every letter is still counted for stats and achievements.

Typing earns XP only. Tiers stay time (0004), and coins come from tiers,
quests, check-ins and boxes as in DAW Battle Pass, so the economy (0013)
carries over: about 550 coins an hour of writing.
