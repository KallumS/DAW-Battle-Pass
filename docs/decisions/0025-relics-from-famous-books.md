# 0025 - Relics from famous books

The user suggested items inspired by famous writers' works, such as Stephen
King's and Edgar Allan Poe's. So some Legendary and Mythic wardrobe pieces
are **relics**: The Raven's Quill, Paul Sheldon's Typewriter (missing its N),
the Overlook Caretaker's Jacket, the Tell-Tale Heart, Room 237's key, the
Deerstalker of Baker Street, the Orient Express Valise - 25 across the
eight slots (`Relics.swift`), from Poe, King, Shelley, Stoker, Doyle,
Dickens, Austen, Christie, Lovecraft, Shirley Jackson, Woolf, Melville and
Emily Brontë.

- **A nod, in our own words.** Each has a name, one line of flavour we wrote
  (a phrase at most borrowed from a public-domain classic), whose books it
  comes from, and a power chosen to suit it (Bartleby "would prefer not
  to", so his pen gives a free daily reroll). Stats roll as for any piece
  of its rarity, so a relic is never simply the best item.
- **Rare enough to matter:** 40% of Legendary pieces, 60% of Mythic ones.
  Lesser pieces never are. A "Literary Relics" trophy counts them.
- The same writers turn up elsewhere in lighter ways: writing challenges
  "very Poe", "very Stephen King", genres like Small-Town Horror, and
  titles "of the House of Usher" or "of Castle Rock".

Living authors' worlds are referenced only by place and object names, never
quoted at length.
