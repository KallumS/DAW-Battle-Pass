# 0023 - Clothes and kit, not frills

The first version leaned hard into "makeup and buying clothes" (her words):
a New Lipstick Pass, a Nail Appointment Pass, a Dream Dress Pass, and a
wardrobe of lipstick, perfume, jewellery, heels and handbags, with titles
like Prose Princess and Draft Duchess. The user then said she's feminine but
a bit of a tomboy, and those three passes in particular had to go.

So the rewards stay about clothes and a little make-up, but everyday ones:

- **Treats:** Game Night (was Lipstick), Gig Tickets (was Nails), New Jacket
  (was Dream Dress), Night In (was Pamper Night), New Trainers (was New
  Shoes), Makeup Restock (was Makeup Haul). Prices unchanged.
- **Wardrobe:** Headphones, Hat, Accessory (watches, rings), Outfit (band
  tees to leather jackets), Shoes (sneakers to combat boots), Bag (backpacks
  and satchels), beside the Pen and Desk Charm.
- **Words:** titles like Prose Pirate and Draft Dragon; epithets, banners and
  season names without the frills.

Story prompts are left alone: a character may still wear red lipstick.
`testRewardsAreNotFrilly` lists the words that may not appear in anything
handed out or sold. She can still add any treat she likes in Settings
(0021).
