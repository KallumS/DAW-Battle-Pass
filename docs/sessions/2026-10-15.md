# Session log - 15 October 2026: Apple's HIG Foundations (1.1)

Asked, across all the user's repositories: bring them in line with Apple's
Human Interface Guidelines, as the new Transient Shuffler is, with the 19
Foundations pages attached. The user chose the full set Transient Shuffler
has. The family's ReaImGui windows (Midi Suggester first, then Variator,
Starting Blocks and its Notation, Midi Catalogue, Good Idea) took all of it;
this window took what a game board drawn at fixed coordinates can take.
Recorded as [0018](../decisions/0018-apples-hig-foundations.md); the
page-by-page review is [`docs/apple-hig-review.md`](../apple-hig-review.md).

## What changed

- **Words at 7:1.** `DIM` `#8A919C` -> `#B0B6C0`. `bp_loot`'s rarities gained
  `text` beside `col` (Poor `#B0B6C0`, Rare `#8FBBFF`, Epic `#C9A6FF`,
  Legendary `#FFA45C`, Mythic `#FF92C9`; Common and Uncommon were already
  over 7:1); the window draws names with `RT(r)`. `PASS_TEXT` `#FFA45C` for
  the pass's orange in words.
- **Reduce Motion** on the Stats page. `bp_fx`: `fx.calm` makes no particles,
  shake, flash, bump or coin flight; floaters made while calm stay put. The
  window: `settle` around every `F.ease` (an entrance lands at its end),
  `wave` for every time-driven `math.sin` (nothing pulses or bobs), `D.rays`
  does not turn, the sleeping z's and the achievement shine are skipped.
- **Toasts stay** until clicked or Esc; four at most. `F.dismiss`; the
  countdown bar and the `life` fields are gone. A click on a toast sets
  `ui.blockClicks`, so the page under it sees none that frame.
- **Keyboard navigation** and its ring `#F2F4F7`.

## What went wrong on the way

- The regex that sent `math.sin(t *` through `wave` also caught `ICON.wave`,
  where `t` is a place along the icon, not time: the wave icon would have been
  drawn flat with Reduce Motion on. Put back by hand.
- The first word-contrast run found three names still in fill colours
  (`RARE` on the Shop's cards, an idea's rarity, a crate's "+ Rare Crate") and
  the crate titles; each now uses `RT`.
- The toast tests first looked for "Equipped", which is also the Gear page's
  heading; they count it now.
- A line clamping `reduceMotion` to true/false in `clampState` changed nothing
  when broken: `fill` already replaces a wrong-typed value. Removed.
