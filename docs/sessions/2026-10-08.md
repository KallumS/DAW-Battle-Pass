# 2026-10-08 - the first version

## Asked for

A battle pass for REAPER: track time in a project and actions, detect idling;
a small reward every fifteen minutes and a big one each hour; endless
procedurally made rewards (coins for a shop of permission passes - takeaway,
movie, a two-hour gaming session - song titles from two words, song themes,
chord progressions, and more); progress saved across projects; daily
check-in, daily and weekly quests, some naming plugins; XP from actions that
unlocks crates; achievements; fancy animations for every bit of progress.
Then, part way through: take inspiration from Fortnite's battle pass, the
Poor-to-Mythic rarity ladder and Diablo's itemization, and look at the
user's other repos.

## What was built

- A first single-file `gfx` draft was thrown away once the sister repos were
  read ([0001](../decisions/0001-a-reascript-in-the-family-shape.md)).
- Six files in the family's shape, about 5,600 lines; six test suites, about
  2,200 more.
- Seasons as calendar months with a 100-tier track that can be previewed;
  tiers on active time, levels on XP; seven rarities; thirteen kinds of
  idea; six gear slots with fourteen affixes and ten powers; titles and
  banners; a shop with daily deals; 28 achievement series.

## What broke on the way

- Swung drum hits on the last sixteenth ran past the end of the pattern.
- Exotic modes on flat keys spelled double flats (Ab Phrygian's Bbb):
  fixed by spelling a key the way Good Idea's `transpose` does.
- A crate's contents could level you up, and the level-up showed first:
  announcements now come before grants
  ([0008](../decisions/0008-nothing-earned-is-lost.md)).
- "Start with REAPER" failed if the Scripts folder didn't exist.
- The container restarted once mid-session, before anything was committed.
  Nothing was lost, but commit early.

## Prices

A simulated fortnight of two-hour sessions came to about 575 coins an active
hour, early achievements included. Passes were roughly doubled from the
first draft so a takeaway is about five hours of music.

## Not done yet

- **Never run inside REAPER.** Everything is tested against mocks written
  from the documented signatures; the ReaImGui calls are the 0.9 API. The
  first real run may need small fixes - look at the draw list calls and
  fonts first.
- The REAPER calls have not been checked against the API page the user
  uploaded for the sister repos.
- No sound effects.
- `index.xml` points at a commit on the working branch; it resolves for
  ReaPack once merged to `main` (merge, don't squash).
