# 2026-10-08 - the first version

## Asked for

A battle pass for REAPER: track time in a project and the actions taken,
and notice when the user is idle; a small reward every fifteen minutes and a
bigger one on the hour; rewards made on the fly so they never run out (coins
for a shop of permission passes - a takeaway, a movie, a two-hour gaming
session - song titles from two words, song themes, chord progressions, and
anything else that can be made procedurally); progress saved across projects;
daily check-in rewards, daily and weekly quests, some about particular
plugins; XP from every action, filling a bar that unlocks loot crates;
achievements; fancy animations for every bit of progress.

Part way through, two additions: take inspiration from Fortnite's battle
pass, the Poor-to-Mythic rarity ladder and Diablo's itemization; and look at
the user's other repos (Good Idea, Starting Blocks, ScaleView for REAPER,
Noterator, Midi Catalogue) for anything useful.

At the end: an architecture record of the big decisions, this log, and a
CLAUDE.md.

## The route

1. **A single-file `gfx` script**, started before the second message: one
   file to copy, no extensions needed. About 400 lines in (config, dates,
   saving) the request to look at the sister repos arrived.
2. **Reading the family.** Every sister repo is a ReaImGui ReaScript with the
   window in one file and pure-Lua engines beside it, tests against a REAPER
   and an ImGui mocked from the API docs, a ReaPack index pinned to commits,
   `docs/decisions` and `docs/sessions`, and the house colours (Good Idea's
   `docs/COLOUR.md`). The draft was deleted and the script rebuilt in that
   shape ([0001](../decisions/0001-a-reascript-in-the-family-shape.md)).
   Good Idea's theory file (ScaleView's roots and scales, Starting Blocks'
   chord table) was copied in unchanged so chords are named the family's
   way; its Park-Miller dice were taken too. Noterator, a JUCE app, had
   nothing to borrow for a ReaScript but its `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, which
   this repo's copies.
3. **The engines, then the window.** `bp_theory`, `bp_loot` (13 kinds of
   idea, gear, cosmetics, crates, the season track), `bp_game` (the rules),
   `bp_fx` (motion), `bp_watch` (REAPER), then the window: a header with the
   banner, level bar and coin counter; eight pages; the stage for rewards;
   the overlay.
4. **The tests**, six suites, and `tools/bite.sh` to prove they bite.
5. **Prices** set from a simulated economy, **docs**, the **ReaPack index**
   pinned to the tested commit, and the pinned links checked: GitHub serves
   all six files at that commit.

## The design, in a paragraph

The fifteen-minute rewards became the tiers of a Fortnite-style season pass:
a calendar month, 100 tiers, each hour's tier big, milestones every ten, a
Mythic finale, bonus tiers for ever, and the whole track visible in advance
because each tier's reward is made from the season and tier number alone.
XP from actions fills a separate level bar, a crate per level
([0004](../decisions/0004-tiers-are-time-levels-are-actions.md)). Seven
rarities, Poor to Mythic. Diablo gave the gear: six studio slots, implicit
stats, affixes that name the item, powers on Legendary and Mythic - none of
which may make a tier come faster
([0006](../decisions/0006-gear-never-shortens-the-clock.md)). The full list of
decisions is in [`../ARCHITECTURE.md`](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## What broke on the way, and what looked broken but wasn't

- **Grammar in the word lists**: "A anxious song", "the gardens still
  shines", "The Satellitess". Fixed with an a/an pass, singular nouns for
  lyrics, and a plural that doesn't double up. `test_loot` checks a/an.
- **Swung drums ran past the end of the pattern**: a swung last sixteenth
  plus its length went over the bar. The note is shortened to fit.
- **Double flats in chord names** (Bbb as a bVI in Db, and Ab Phrygian's Bbb
  and Fb). Fixed at the root: a key is spelled the way that gives its scale
  the fewest accidentals, Good Idea's `transpose` rule, so Ab Phrygian
  becomes G# Phrygian. A guard in `spell` stays as a backstop.
- **A level-up jumped ahead of the crate that caused it.** `test_ui` opened
  the starter crate and got the level-up screen first, because the crate's
  XP was granted before the crate was announced. Now a tier or crate is
  announced first and granted second
  ([0008](../decisions/0008-nothing-earned-is-lost.md)).
- **"Start with REAPER" failed when the Scripts folder didn't exist.** It now
  makes the folder.
- **Fractional seconds from the test clock** reached `os.date` and raised.
  Real `os.time()` is always whole, but `G.tick` now rounds it down anyway.
- **Looked broken, wasn't**: `test_ui` reported only one reward collected in
  an hour. The test only clicked "Collect" and level-up screens ("Later")
  were queued between rewards - correct behaviour. Likewise the Redeem
  button "missing" after a purchase: the purchase's achievement had levelled
  the player up, and the level-up rightly had the stage.
- **Test rules that were too strict**: `%` in "swing 62%" counted as a
  leftover template mark; chord symbols like "A Major" tripped the a/an
  rule; a test click on "Star" matched the "Starred" filter. The rules were
  narrowed, and exact button labels now win over prefixes.
- **Two bites that didn't bite at first.** A save able to run code "failed"
  only by killing the test process; it now checks that nothing outside the
  save was touched. Removing `spell`'s guard changed nothing, because key
  spelling had already been fixed at the root; the bite now breaks the root
  instead.
- **The container restarted once**, killing a `bite.sh` run, before anything
  had been committed. Nothing was lost - the files were on disk - but the
  first commit followed straight away. Commit early.

## Prices

A simulation of ten players, two hours a day for five days, checking in,
finishing quests and opening every crate, came to about 575 coins an active
hour (early achievements included). The first prices had a takeaway two
hours away; passes were roughly doubled so it is about five
([0013](../decisions/0013-prices-from-a-simulated-economy.md)). Ten hours
of play reached level 19 and tier 39.

## Not done yet

- **Never run inside REAPER.** Everything is tested against mocks written
  from the documented signatures, and the ReaImGui calls are the 0.9 API.
  The first real run may need small fixes - look first at the draw list
  calls (`DrawList_AddTextEx` with a font, `PathArcTo`), fonts
  (`CreateFont` with `FontFlags_Bold`, `PushFont`) and
  `GetForegroundDrawList`.
- The REAPER calls haven't been checked against the API page the user
  uploaded for the sister repos (REAPER 7.79).
- Without js_ReaScriptAPI, moving the mouse in another app counts as being
  active.
- No sound effects.
- No screenshot or preview image in the README, as the sister repos have.
