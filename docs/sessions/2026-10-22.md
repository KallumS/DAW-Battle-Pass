# 2026-10-22 - 1.2: the final sweep of Apple's Foundations

The family's final HIG sweep. An earlier attempt had been stopped part-way
and stashed ("Unfinished HIG final sweep"): it had already turned the whole
board into points drawn at a scale - `D.*`, `dtext`, `at`, `hit`, `mouseIn`
and `origin` - with the View menu, the zoom keys, `pushSize`, wrapping rows,
the ink bar, lighter popups and "Click Again". It was applied, finished and
committed, then the stash dropped.

What finishing took:

- **The fonts' measure was off by the text size**: each font recorded its
  base size, not the size it was made at, so at 200% every measurement was
  doubled. `px` is now the pixel size.
- The tests had never seen it: the mock lacked the new calls (size
  constraints, style variables, popups, focus, `GetOS`), and five labels had
  changed to title style.
- Added: the title naming the page, the minimum size grown with the text,
  menu keys `#BFC5CE`, the scrollbar grab and resize grip at 3:1, Esc minding
  an open menu, `textSize` in `G.newState`.
- Tests: the family's `tests/hig_mock.lua`, a no-overflow check on every page,
  the welcome and a crate at 100% and 200% in a roomy and the smallest window
  (the mock respects clip rects, since the pass's track scrolls inside one),
  the title, Esc, no timer, the theme's greys.

Sabotages: eighteen, all bite. The first Esc test did not - the welcome
ignores Esc anyway - and was moved to an armed button.
