# 2026-10-22 - Writing Battle Pass

## Asked for

A friend wanted one for her writing: repurpose DAW Battle Pass for a woman in
her thirties who writes, with rewards tailored to make-up and buying clothes
(her examples), XP for every letter typed but not scaled too highly, and -
the big difference - a standalone app for an Apple silicon Mac.

## The route

1. **Choosing what to build it in** ([0018](../decisions/0018-the-writer-is-a-native-mac-app.md)).
   LÖVE would have reused the Lua engines and been screenshot-able here, but
   counting typing in other apps, a menu bar clock and login items would all
   have gone through LuaJIT's FFI. Swift and SwiftUI won; the rules were
   ported to a pure-Swift `PassCore` in the Lua engines' shape.
2. **No Swift here.** The container can't download a Swift toolchain
   (download.swift.org is refused by the proxy), and Swift on Linux couldn't
   build AppKit anyway. GitHub Actions on `macos-15` became the compiler,
   test runner and packager, with a `--snapshots` mode that draws every page
   to PNG.
3. **The engine**, retuned: letters and words from a key-count tap
   ([0019](../decisions/0019-typing-is-counted-never-recorded.md)), 0.1 XP a
   letter capped at 30 a minute ([0020](../decisions/0020-letters-are-a-top-up.md)),
   fifteen-minute sprints, word milestones, a wardrobe of eight slots (pen,
   lipstick, perfume, jewellery, outfit, shoes, handbag, desk charm), thirteen
   kinds of writing prompt, writerly titles and seasons, and fourteen treats
   she can edit ([0021](../decisions/0021-her-treats-are-hers-to-edit.md)).
4. **The app**: eight pages, the stage, toasts and particles, the menu bar
   ([0022](../decisions/0022-closing-the-window-keeps-counting.md)), the
   welcome, the permission flow, an icon drawn with PIL.
5. **CI**, then fixes from its compiler. Four rounds: (1) one arithmetic
   line "too complex to type-check in reasonable time" - every similar line
   was split into named parts, and word picks got a non-generic
   `Dice.word` so long sentences type-check fast; (2) everything compiled,
   and the content test caught Uncommon story prompts with no second line,
   reported thousands of times - the test now reports each broken rule once;
   (3) all green: 46 tests, the app built and signed, 18 snapshots drawn;
   (4) fixes from reading the Demo output (below).
6. **Reading the Demo output** in the log found what the tests hadn't:
   settings read "An a lost-property office" (the place list already had its
   article), male characters were given "she" and "her", and a full stop
   followed a quote that had already ended its sentence. All three fixed and
   now tested.
7. **Reading the snapshots** (decoded from the log): the stage, the slip,
   the check-in and level-up looked right first time. The pages were blank
   - `ImageRenderer` can't see into a `ScrollView` - so snapshot mode lays
   them out unscrolled (`HScroll` does the same for sideways rows). Once
   visible they showed one real bug: each quest card's rarity strip was a
   shape in the row, which stretches to whatever height it's offered; it's
   an overlay now. And "Open 1 gift boxes", "Write for 1 hours": `agree`
   makes them singular.

## What looked broken and wasn't

- **"GitHub isn't running anything."** No workflow run appeared for half an
  hour and I asked the user to switch Actions on; it was already on. The
  workflow's `push:` trigger listed `tags:` and `paths:` but no `branches:`,
  and a push trigger that names only tags ignores every branch push. Adding
  `branches: ["**"]` fixed it.

## The economy

The Economy test (two hours a day for a fortnight, quests claimed, boxes
opened, Poor and Common gear sold) measures about **630 coins an hour** and
level 38 after 28 hours. So: a fancy coffee is half an hour of writing, a
new lipstick two and a half hours, a new top about four, a makeup haul six,
a shopping spree about 24.

## Seeing it without a Mac

Artifacts can't be downloaded from the container (the storage host is
refused), but job logs can be read, so CI prints each snapshot as a small
base64 JPEG between `SNAP-BEGIN`/`SNAP-END` markers
(`tools/print_snapshots.sh`). Decode them from the log to look.

## The icon

Drawn with PIL in the container (`writer/tools/draw_icon.py`): a 1024px
rounded square, plum to magenta, a gold fountain-pen nib, a lemon sparkle.
`make_app.sh` makes every size with `sips` and `iconutil`.

## Later: less girly, and Miderator

The user said their friend is feminine but a bit of a tomboy: the lipstick,
dress and nail passes had to change. They went, along with the rest of the
frills (0023), and a test now keeps them out. The user also pointed at
Miderator (their JUCE piano-roll app): its .dmg packaging, install note and
Apple-guideline habits (7:1 text, Reduce Motion) came across (0024).
Then: relics from famous books - Poe and Stephen King first, then a dozen
other writers - as some Legendary and Mythic pieces (0025).

## Not done yet

- Ad-hoc signed: first launch needs Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, and

  Developer ID and notarisation would remove both.
- Google Docs counts only by counting the whole browser.
